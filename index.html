What this is

An immersive ritual system, grounded in a sacred place.

The project translates the Forum’s theme — uniting intelligences — into a living environment set within the sacred landscape of Fuji Sanctuary in Japan.

193 participants, each representing a different country, come together to enter a shared system — not as observers, but as active agents.

Through their interactions, decisions, and relationships, collective intelligence is not explained. It emerges in real time.

Participatory

Everyone is an active contributor. The experience unfolds through choices, reflection, attention, and embodied collaboration.

Living System

The world responds to the collective state of the group. Nothing is merely decorative; every action influences the field.

Multi-Intelligence

Human, artificial, nature, and cosmic intelligences interact inside one ritual architecture.

The global challenge

We are not short on intelligence. We are short on alignment.

Humanity now lives among powerful forms of intelligence — human creativity, AI systems, ecological knowledge, and spiritual longing. Yet our systems still fragment, extract, and fail because these intelligences rarely act in coherence.


Fuji Sanctuary creates a safe, beautiful, and rigorous environment where participants can rehearse a different possibility: not control, not optimization, but collective coherence.

What happens

Participants enter a world that reflects them back.

The narrative is not the whole project. It is the emotional language that lets participants feel the system.


The Sun begins to fade. Mother Earth weakens. Participants are mirrored as symbolic elemental beings and asked to act. Their choices reshape the world. When fragmented action creates imbalance, they must reorganize into a more coherent collective.

01

Enter the threshold

Participants arrive, slow down, and cross from ordinary reality into a shared ritual field.

02

Receive a living mirror

Through reflection, AI, or biofeedback, each participant receives a symbolic form — an avatar or elemental identity that reveals their role in the system.

03

Act from partial intelligence

Groups make decisions from their own perspective. They may be sincere, creative, and intelligent — yet incomplete.

04

Witness consequence

The world responds. The group sees how individual or isolated choices can create system-level imbalance.

05

Reorganize through difference

Participants shift into new constellations, learning that unity does not require sameness. Coherence emerges through relation.

06

Restore the field

Through embodied collaboration, sound, light, movement, and intention, the living system returns to balance.
